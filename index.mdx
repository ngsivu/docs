## Get up and running in three steps

Follow this onboarding flow to go from zero to a fully connected Google integration as quickly as possible.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your Google account">
    Sign up at [google.com](https://google.com) and complete your profile. If your organization already uses Google Workspace, ask your administrator to add you as a user. Once you're in, verify your email address and enable two-factor authentication to keep your account secure.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure your workspace and generate an API key">
    Head to the **Google Cloud Console**, create a new project, and enable the APIs your application needs. Navigate to **APIs & Services → Credentials** and generate your first API key or OAuth 2.0 client ID. Store your credentials somewhere safe — you'll need them in the next step.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make your first API call and explore the dashboard">
    Use your API key to authenticate a test request and confirm everything is wired up correctly. Then explore the Google Cloud Console dashboard to review usage metrics, set up billing alerts, and browse the full catalog of available services. You're ready to build.
  </Step>
</Steps>
